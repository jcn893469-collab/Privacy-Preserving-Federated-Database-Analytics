Privacy-Preserving Federated Analytics

Allows distributed healthcare & enterprise organizations to collaboratively analyze sensitive records without centralizing raw data. Raw CSV files are processed entirely in the local client environment with Z-score anomaly detection, data clipping, and calibrated Gaussian Differential Privacy. Only protected aggregates and RDP privacy loss bounds are persisted in the PostgreSQL database.
